Guardrails

Bots propose; people approve. No client-facing message leaves without a named approver.
POPIA: employee names and medical outcomes stay inside the portal and MCO. Emails carry counts and dates only.
Every allocation follows the parent-child structure: consultant owns, team lead sees, manager approves, director audits.
Every bot action is reversible from the task's Bot log within 24 hours.
