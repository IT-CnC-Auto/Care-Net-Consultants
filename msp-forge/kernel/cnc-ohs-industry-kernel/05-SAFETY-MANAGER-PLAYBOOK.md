# 05 Safety Manager Playbook

**Audience:** Client Safety Managers and Care Net Method practitioners  
**Stance:** Experienced Safety Manager know-how with **ISO 45001 awareness** as management-system discipline. ISO 45001 helps you organise PDCA, leadership, and documented information. It is **not** a substitute for South African statute (OHS Act, agent regulations, COIDA, EEA s7, POPIA purpose limitation).  
**Kernel date:** 23 September 2026  

---

## 1. Opening a new workplace / Method assessment flow

1. **Scoping call (Adult-to-Adult).** Confirm activities, headcount bands, shifts, contractors, and whether mining / rail / radiation / PrDP regimes apply.  
2. **Pick industry pack(s)** from the 17 (section 2). Multi-activity employers get a composite Plan.  
3. **Document harvest.** Appointments, existing HIRA, hygiene surveys, MSP, COIDA letter of good standing, previous medicals.  
4. **Walk the workplace.** Verify that paperwork matches reality (noise zones, chemical stores, bio risks, heights).  
5. **HIRA refresh** using `TEMPLATE-HIRA-register.md`.  
6. **Exposure confirmation.** Commission or review AIA / competent person measurements before locking protocols.  
7. **Draft MSP** with OMP (`TEMPLATE-medical-surveillance-plan.md`).  
8. **Appointments and induction hygiene** (`TEMPLATE-legal-appointments-checklist.md`, induction record).  
9. **Issue Method Plan cover** (`TEMPLATE-industry-plan-cover.md`).  
10. **Book Care Net delivery** (clinic or mobile) against the MSP calendar.  
11. **Close the loop** after first cycle: update HIRA, file COFs, retain records.

Never start at step 7 with a protocol shopping list.

## 2. How to choose which of the 17 industry packs

| Question | Prefer pack |
| --- | --- |
| Is the work mining under MHSA? | **9 Mining** (then fetch MHSA before High claims) |
| Is the main activity construction work under CR 2014? | **3 Construction** |
| Healthcare clinical / lab bio risk? | **6 Healthcare and laboratories** |
| Process chemicals / refining? | **11 Petrochemical** or **8 Manufacturing** (chemical) |
| Mostly offices / contact centre? | **10 Office and professional** |
| Drivers with PrDP? | **15 Transport** (plus PrDP catalogue) |
| Mixed municipal services? | **5 Government and municipal** with streams split |

If unsure, map **tasks**, not the client's marketing sector label. A "retail" DC with forklifts and night shift is closer to logistics/manufacturing light than to a boutique store.

## 3. HIRA → exposure confirmation → protocol selection

```
HIRA identifies hazard
        |
        v
Is exposure plausible for this cohort?
        |
        +-- No --> Do not add protocol; keep awareness training if needed
        |
        +-- Yes --> Measure / competent assessment (noise, agents, bio risk)
        |
        v
Compare to action levels / OELs / NER noise-rating / HBA risk group
        |
        v
OMP specifies MSP content and intervals
        |
        v
CNC schedules protocols from the 30-list that the MSP names
```

**Forbidden pattern:** "Industry 8 manufacturing gets all 41-role medicals."  
**Required pattern:** Cohort sheets with protocol ticks tied to HIRA refs.

## 5. Audit trail before a DoEL / inspector visit

**Forty-eight hour grab pack (adapt to site):**

1. 16(1)/16(2) and HS rep appointments (accepted, current).  
2. Latest HIRA + evidence of employee consultation.  
3. Noise and physical-agents programme under **NER/PAR 2024** (not repealed NIHL 2003 / Env 1987 text).  
4. Audiometry quality evidence if noise applies (CoP: competent person, calibration, SANS 10182 environment cert for booths/mobiles).  
5. Chemical inventory, SDS, HCA risk assessments, biomonitoring where required.  
6. HBA risk assessments and **written medical protocols** if bio applies.  
7. MSP approved by OMP + sample COFs.  
8. Induction records for sampled employees.  
9. Incident register + GAR notifications + CompEasy claim samples.  
10. Contractor file index if construction ongoing.  

Safety Manager behaviour: be factual, produce the document, do not argue statute from memory if unsure. Escalate (section 6).

## 6. When to escalate

| Escalate to | When |
| --- | --- |
| **OMP** | Abnormal findings; unfit/restriction decisions; vulnerable employee under NER; written medical protocol design; OD likelihood opinions |
| **Occupational hygienist / AIA** | Measurement design; OEL exceedance; asbestos air monitoring; noise zoning disputes |
| **Attorney** | Inspector enforcement notices; disputed EEA s7 medical testing; POPIA complaints; MHSA vs OHS Act coverage fights; fatal incidents |
| **Watch Bee / research** | Instruments marked catalogue / UNVERIFIED in `06-GAPS-AND-NEXT.md` |

## 7. Interface with Care Net clinics and mobile units

7.1 Care Net footprint (MD brief memory): about **4 fixed clinics** and **16 mobile units**, supporting high monthly medical volumes. Treat exact sites as operational data; Midrand is a known brand presence. Do not invent a national site list in Method PDFs.

7.2 Mobiles and NER CoP: if audiometry is delivered from mobiles, plan electro-acoustic calibration and biological checks after moves, plus SANS 10182 environment certification. This is a common inspector finding.

7.3 Booking inputs Care Net needs: MSP cohort list, HIRA refs, purchase order, employee roster with job categories, prior baselines where exit/periodic depends on them.

7.4 Outputs you should expect: COF outcomes, cover notes, referral flags, quality-controlled test data for OMP sign-off, and filing packs that match your retention tree.

7.5 Payment: employer pays occupational health services per HPCSA guidance. Do not put employees in the middle of fee disputes.

## 8. ISO 45001 awareness (optional overlay)

If the client runs ISO 45001:

- Map Kernel templates to documented information controls.  
- Use management review to track NER/PAR transition evidence after 6 September 2026.  
- Keep legal register rows pointing at **Gazette texts**, not only ISO clauses.  

ISO certification without NER-compliant audiometry still fails an OHS inspection on a noise site.
