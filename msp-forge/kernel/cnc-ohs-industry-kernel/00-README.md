## 1. What this Kernel is

1.1 This Kernel is a **Safety Manager and Method** working pack. It maps seventeen industry engagement packs and thirty medical screening protocols to South African occupational health and safety instruments that Care Net already holds on disk (or catalogues honestly where the body is not local).

1.2 It is **not** a new software product, clinic EMR, or Lighthouse module. It is markdown documentation that a Safety Manager, Method consultant, OHN, or OMP can read, print, and adapt after professional review.

1.3 Care Net Consultants screens **fitness for work**. Diagnosis stays with the reviewing OMP. The employer remains responsible for payment of occupational health services in line with HPCSA guidance. Findings and referrals are clinical acts of the reviewing OMP, not of Method marketing copy.

## 4. Currency note (NER / PAR)

4.1 **Noise Exposure Regulations, 2024** (GN 5953, GG 52226, 6 March 2025) and the incorporated **Code of Practice for Audiometry** are operative. The Noise-Induced Hearing Loss Regulations, 2003 (GNR 307, GG 24967) were **repealed effective 6 September 2026** (NER regulation 18: 18 months after promulgation).

4.2 **Physical Agents Regulations, 2024** (GN 5952, GG 52226, 6 March 2025) are operative. The Environmental Regulations for Workplaces, 1987 were **repealed effective 6 September 2026** on the same 18-month structure.

4.3 Departmental media sometimes said "5 September 2026". Authoritative text is the Gazette arithmetic (**6 September 2026**). See also `/workspace/ohs-research/OHS-Readiness-Brief-2026-08-26.md` and INDEX section 1.4.

4.4 Lead Regulations, 2001 remain in force. Draft Lead Regulation 2024 (GN R4437, GG 50203) is **draft only** in this pack (not promulgated as at INDEX date).

## 5. SANDBOX and professional review

5.1 This Kernel is a **sandbox** cognitive aid. It is **not legal advice** and **not a clinical protocol signed by an OMP**.

5.2 Before client issue or Method commercial use: (a) OMP review of medical surveillance and certificate-of-fitness pathways; (b) attorney review of appointment and liability wording; (c) confirmation that any industry-specific statute marked **catalogue / UNVERIFIED** has been fetched or expressly accepted as gap.

5.3 Do not stamp any template or letter "POPIA compliant", "Lighthouse compliant", or similar. Use purpose limitation and retention language instead (see `04-TEMPLATES/TEMPLATE-POPIA-OH-records-purpose-note.md`).

## 6. Helena voice note (Care Net)

6.1 Written client-facing templates use a **warm-clinical Helena** tone with **PIF Adult-to-Adult** transactions: clear, respectful, no parental scolding, no child-pleasing fluff.

6.2 Spelling: South African / British English (**organise**, **colour**, **programme**, **licence** as noun where applicable).

6.3 Typography: **no em dashes (U+2014) and no en dashes (U+2013)** anywhere in this Kernel. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or "to" for ranges.

6.4 Footer stance (every medical-facing template): Care Net focuses on screening fitness for work and does not provide medical diagnoses. Findings and referrals sit with the reviewing OMP. The employer is always responsible for payment (HPCSA guidance).
