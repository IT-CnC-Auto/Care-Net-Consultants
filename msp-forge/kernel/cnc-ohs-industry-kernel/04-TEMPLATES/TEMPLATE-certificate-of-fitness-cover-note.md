## Cover note

Dear **________________**,

Thank you for attending your occupational health appointment on **____ / ____ / ________** at **________________** (clinic / mobile unit).

Care Net screened your **fitness for work** in relation to the job and exposures your employer asked us to consider. We do **not** provide medical diagnoses through this occupational screening service. If anything needs medical diagnosis or treatment, the reviewing Occupational Medicine Practitioner (OMP) will explain the next step and any referral.

### What your employer receives

Your employer receives a fitness outcome appropriate to the law and the agreed Medical Surveillance Plan, for example:

- Fit for the stated work
- Fit with restrictions / conditions
- Temporarily unfit
- Unfit

The employer does **not** automatically receive your full clinical notes. Health information is handled as special personal information for the occupational health purpose described in our purpose note.
