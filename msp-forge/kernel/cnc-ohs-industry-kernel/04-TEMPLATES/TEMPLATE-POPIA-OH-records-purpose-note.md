## 2. Purpose of processing (Adult-to-Adult plain language)

We process your health and identity information to:

1. Assess and report **fitness for work** against job demands and confirmed workplace exposures.  
2. Run **medical surveillance** required or appropriate under the OHS Act and agent regulations (for example NER, PAR, HCA, HBA, Asbestos, Lead, Construction Regulations).  
3. Provide the employer with a **fitness outcome** (and restrictions if needed), not a full clinical story.  
4. Support **statutory incident / occupational disease** reporting and COIDA processes when applicable.  
5. Keep **occupational health records** for the retention periods the OHS framework requires.

We do **not** process this information for unrelated marketing.

## 5. Retention (including 40-year OH context)

Some occupational health programmes, notably under **NER 2024** and **PAR 2024**, require long retention of specified records (**40 years** is the planning figure used in Care Net Value-Chain mapping for those programmes). Other agent regulations may set parallel duties. POPIA storage limitation must be read **together with** these OHS retention duties: keep what the law requires, for as long as it requires, with security safeguards, then dispose safely.
