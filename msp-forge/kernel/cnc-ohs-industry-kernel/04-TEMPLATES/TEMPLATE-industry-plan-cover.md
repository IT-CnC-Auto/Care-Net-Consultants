## What this Plan is (and is not)

**Is:** A regulator-aware engagement plan that starts from **your** HIRA, maps the Acts and regulations Care Net can cite from its instrument pack, and selects medical protocols only after exposure confirmation.

**Is not:** A generic template that copies every protocol in the industry onto every employee. Not a claim of POPIA or Lighthouse "compliance". Not a substitute for MHSA, NRTA, RSR, or radiation licence text where those regimes apply and are still catalogue-only in our Kernel.

## Currency banner

From **6 September 2026**, Noise Exposure Regulations, 2024 and Physical Agents Regulations, 2024 are the operative noise and physical-agents regimes. NIHL Regulations, 2003 and Environmental Regulations for Workplaces, 1987 are repealed. This Plan uses NER/PAR language.
