## 1. Purpose

This record shows that the employee received information, instruction and training appropriate to workplace hazards, in line with Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993 duties and applicable agent regulations (NER, PAR, HCA, Asbestos, Lead, HBA, Construction Regulations as relevant). Training supports safe work; it does not replace medical surveillance.
