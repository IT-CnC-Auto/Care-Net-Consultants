## How to use

Keep one index at the front of the contractor OHS file (hard copy or controlled electronic folder). Mark each item **Present / Missing / NA**. Missing items block induction until resolved (Adult-to-Adult: explain why, do not shame).

Medical certificates: Care Net may supply construction fitness certificates under the MSP. Clinical detail stays with the OMP pathway.
