## 2. Purpose (Adult-to-Adult)

We agree this MSP exists to protect employee health in relation to **workplace exposures confirmed on the HIRA**, to support lawful fitness-for-work decisions, and to meet duties under the OHS Act and applicable agent regulations (NER, PAR, HCA, HBA, Asbestos, Lead, Construction Regulations as relevant). It is not a general wellness package and it is not diagnostic primary care.

Special personal information will be processed for this occupational health purpose under POPIA conditions applicable to health professionals. See `TEMPLATE-POPIA-OH-records-purpose-note.md`. Do not stamp this plan "POPIA compliant".

## 5. Certificates of fitness and confidentiality

5.1 The employer receives a **fitness outcome** (fit / fit with restrictions / temporarily unfit / unfit) appropriate to the regulation, not the full clinical record.

5.2 Clinical notes remain under OH professional control subject to POPIA and HPCSA confidentiality guidance.

5.3 Restrictions are written so supervisors can implement controls without receiving diagnoses.

---

## 6. Record retention

Noise and physical-agents programmes under NER/PAR: plan for **40-year** retention of required records. Parallel agent rules may also apply. Retention schedules must be written; lawful basis explained to employees (purpose note).
