## CNC clinic reminder

For every protocol delivered by Care Net clinics or mobile units: document indication (HIRA reference), consent / purpose under POPIA language (no "compliant" stamp), OMP review of abnormal findings, and employer certificate-of-fitness outcome separate from full clinical notes.
