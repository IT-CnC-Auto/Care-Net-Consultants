# 02 Industry Matrix (17 industries)

**Source of industry list and role counts:** Care Net Method "Your Plan" marketing screen (user-attached).  
**Rule:** Do not invent role names beyond subindustry labels given. Role counts are as supplied.  
**Protocol labels:** **often** = commonly triggered once industry HIRA is typical; **if exposure confirmed by HIRA** = only after competent assessment.

Footer stance: Care Net screens fitness for work; diagnosis stays with reviewing OMP; employer pays (HPCSA guidance).

---

## 1. Agriculture and forestry

- **Subindustries / roles:** Crop farming, livestock, forestry - **19 roles** (source count; names not listed on screen)
- **Primary hazards (Safety Manager view):** Machinery noise and vibration; pesticides and agrochemicals; zoonoses from livestock; dust and organic dust; heat and solar UV; manual handling; chainsaw / forestry trauma; confined spaces (silos); lone work
- **Primary Acts / regs from pack:** OHS Act 85/1993; HCA GN R280; HBA 2022; NER 2024 (if noise zones); PAR 2024 (heat / vibration); GAR 2003; COIDA; Lead 2001 only if lead exposure confirmed
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often:** Audiometry (noise machinery); Cholinesterase monitoring for pesticide exposure; Zoonosis surveillance per written medical protocol; Heat stress tolerance assessment; Musculoskeletal and ergonomic assessment; Dermatological screen (agrochemicals / UV); Vision screening with colour vision and UV skin surveillance
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Spirometry; Respiratory symptom questionnaire; Biological monitoring for specific chemical agents; Confined space medical; Night work medical examination; Biological agent surveillance per written medical protocol
- **Appointments / docs:** 16.1 / 16.2; HS representative(s); first aider; competent persons for HIRA and chemical control; pesticide applicator competence records; medical surveillance plan; incident / OD files
- **Method Plan notes:** Regulator framework is OHS Act agent regs plus agriculture-specific HIRA, not a generic "farm medical" template. Pesticide cholinesterase and zoonosis pathways need written OMP protocols. Forestry noise must follow NER/CoP from 6 Sep 2026, not repealed NIHL 2003 text.

## 2. Cleaning and hygiene

- **Subindustries / roles:** Commercial cleaning, specialised hygiene - **10 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Wet work and dermatitis; cleaning chemicals (HCA); biological agents (body fluids, waste); slip/fall; shift / night work; asbestos disturbance if untrained ACM work; ergonomic strain
- **Primary Acts / regs:** OHS Act; HCA; HBA 2022; Asbestos Abatement 2020 if ACM; PAR (night / physical); GAR; COIDA; EEA s7 for pre-placement
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often:** Dermatological screen; Biological agent surveillance per written medical protocol; Musculoskeletal and ergonomic assessment; Hepatitis B immunity verification and vaccination pathway (specialised hygiene / body fluids)
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Occupational chemical exposure medical assessment; Biological monitoring for specific chemical agents; Night work medical examination; Respiratory symptom questionnaire; Occupational tuberculosis screening (high-risk settings)
- **Appointments / docs:** 16.1/16.2; HS rep; first aider; chemical inventory / SDS file; HBA risk assessment; induction records; contractor OHS file if on client sites
- **Method Plan notes:** Cleaning is agent-driven (HCA + HBA), not "office light medical". Specialised hygiene on healthcare or lab sites borrows healthcare bio protocols after HIRA.

## 3. Construction

- **Subindustries / roles:** Building, civils, demolition, electrical, roadworks - **31 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Falls from heights; noise; dust (silica on civils/demolition); asbestos on demolition / refurb; lead paint; vibration (tools); heat; confined spaces; electrical; mobile plant; manual handling
- **Primary Acts / regs:** **Construction Regulations 2014** (primary); OHS Act; NER; PAR; HCA; Asbestos Abatement 2020; Lead 2001 if applicable; GAR; COIDA
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often:** General construction fitness under CR medical fitness duties; Audiometry; Heights medical with vertigo and balance screen; Musculoskeletal and ergonomic assessment; Vision screening; Heat stress tolerance assessment; Lifting machine operator certificate of fitness (plant operators); Vibration and musculoskeletal screen; General medical for electrical work (electrical trades)
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Spirometry; Respiratory symptom questionnaire; Chest X-ray per silica protocol (**catalogue / MHSA or silica instrument verify** if mine-adjacent; otherwise OMP/HCA asbestos/silica exposure pathway); Confined space medical; Blood lead / lead clinical; Dermatological screen; Night work medical
- **Appointments / docs:** Client / agent / contractor appointments under CR; **CHSO** (construction health and safety officer) / construction regulations appointments as applicable; 16.1/16.2 on employer side; HS reps; first aider; fire fighting where required; contractor OHS file; medical certificates of fitness for construction work
- **Method Plan notes:** Method must open with CR 2014 appointments and contractor file index, not a generic manufacturing pack. Silica imaging: do **not** invent a Gazette; map to confirmed exposure + OMP protocol and catalogue MHSA/silica instruments where relevant.

## 4. Education

- **Subindustries / roles:** Schools/ECD, tertiary/training - **10 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Infectious disease (ECD); ergonomics (admin/teaching); stress / violence interface; laboratory chemicals in tertiary; workshop noise in TVET; asbestos in older buildings; food handling in hostels
- **Primary Acts / regs:** OHS Act; GAR; HBA (infection); HCA (labs/workshops); Asbestos inventory duties if ACM; NER/PAR if workshops; EEA s7; POPIA for learner/staff health data context
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often:** General fitness where inherent requirements exist; Food handler fitness assessment (hostel / catering staff); Biological agent surveillance / TB screening **if** HIRA (ECD / nursing training)
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Audiometry (workshops); Spirometry / chemical medicals (labs); Dermatological screen; Night work (campus security)
- **Appointments / docs:** 16.1/16.2; HS reps; first aider; asbestos inventory / AMP if applicable; HBA risk assessment for ECD illness policies
- **Method Plan notes:** Avoid over-medicalising teachers. Anchor on HIRA and EEA s7 lawful testing. ECD bio risk is real; document written protocols.

## 5. Government and municipal

- **Subindustries / roles:** Admin, public works, emergency/traffic - **16 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Field crew noise/dust/heat; wastewater biohazards; traffic / emergency stress and shift work; workshop chemicals; construction-like public works hazards; driving
- **Primary Acts / regs:** OHS Act; GAR; Construction Regs when public works is construction; HCA; HBA; NER; PAR; PrDP catalogue for traffic officers / drivers; COIDA
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often (field / emergency):** Audiometry; Vision screening; Night work medical examination; Musculoskeletal assessment; Heat stress; PrDP statutory medical and vision screen **if** professional driving
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Biological agent surveillance (wastewater); Confined space; Heights; Chemical exposure medicals; Spirometry
  - **Admin stream:** Often limited to EEA-compliant pre-placement / inherent requirement only
- **Appointments / docs:** 16.1/16.2; HS committees (larger sites); first aider; public works contractor files; emergency response medical programme under OMP
- **Method Plan notes:** Split admin vs field vs emergency streams inside one Method Plan. Do not apply mining or construction full battery to clerical roles.

## 9. Mining

- **Subindustries / roles:** Gold, coal, platinum, chrome, diamond, quarrying - **35 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Dust / silica; noise; heat underground; vibration; diesel particulate; chemical process plants; confined spaces; radiation (NORM in some commodities); trauma; shift work
- **Primary Acts / regs:** **Mine Health and Safety Act 29 of 1996 + regulations (catalogue / verify DMRE)** as primary for mines; OHS Act agent regs may apply at edges / non-mine activities; NER/PAR/HCA local texts still informative for surface analogies but **do not invent MHSA section quotes**
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often (mining practice; legal basis catalogue):** Mine certificate of fitness examination; Dust disease examination battery for mine workers; Chest X-ray per silica protocol; Heat tolerance screening for hot underground workings; Audiometry; Spirometry; Respiratory symptom questionnaire; Vision screening
  - **If HIRA / mine CoP confirms:** Vibration screen; Biological monitoring; Radiation worker surveillance; Confined space; Night work
- **Appointments / docs:** MHSA appointments (mine manager, occupational medical practitioner as required under MHSA framework: **verify**); mine medical station records; DMRE/inspectorate submission rules **verify**
- **Method Plan notes:** **Critical:** This Kernel marks MHSA as **catalogue-only**. Method Plans for mines must be built after fetching MHSA + regulations + silica/dust CoPs. Do not sell an OHS Act-only pack as mine compliance.

## 10. Office and professional

- **Subindustries / roles:** Corporate offices, contact centres - **10 roles**
- **Primary hazards:** Ergonomics / DSE; psychosocial / contact centre voice strain; indoor air quality; sedentary risk; limited chemical exposure (cleaners/contractors)
- **Primary Acts / regs:** OHS Act; GAR; EEA s7; POPIA; PAR (IAQ / illumination interface); NER only if noise HIRA (rare for open office)
- **Typical medical protocols:**
  - **Often:** Musculoskeletal and ergonomic assessment (where MSP justifies); Vision screening (DSE / inherent requirement)
  - **If HIRA confirms:** Night work medical (contact centre nights); Audiometry (only if noise action levels)
  - **Avoid:** Full chemical / mining batteries without exposure evidence
- **Appointments / docs:** 16.1/16.2; HS reps; first aider; emergency evacuation; pre-placement limited by EEA s7
- **Method Plan notes:** Lightest pack. Method value is lawful testing design and POPIA purpose notes, not maximal medicals.

## Cross-industry Method reminder

1. Choose the industry pack from the client's **actual** activities (multi-industry employers get a composite Plan).  
2. Run HIRA → exposure confirmation → protocol selection.  
3. Appointments and training documents are statutory culture; medicals do not replace them.  
4. Mining, rail, radiation, PrDP, and firearm medicals need catalogue instruments fetched before High-confidence legal claims.
