# South African Health and Safety File Pricing: Market Research (2024 to 2026)

Prepared for Care Net Consultants. Retrieved 23 September 2026.

## Method and a limit on it

- I ran about 75 web searches. Every page fetch failed: the sandbox's network egress proxy blocked WebFetch for every South African domain I tried (conrisk, safetyfile.co.za, constructionsafetyfile, rapidsafetyfiles, mysafetyfile, saiosh and others), and I was not allowed to use curl. **So every figure below comes from the search engine's text snippets of the named page, not from reading the page myself.** Each figure comes from a snippet of the cited URL, but you should open each URL and check it before publishing, especially the VAT status and whether a price is a promotion.
- "Retrieved" means 23 Sep 2026 for every row. Most vendor pages show no publication date. Where a date was visible, I give it.
- VAT column: "excl" or "incl" only where the source says so. Otherwise "not stated".
- Type codes:
  - **SVC**: a person compiles the file for you.
  - **AUTO**: software or a form generates a site-specific file.
  - **TPL**: a do-it-yourself template pack. This is not a compiled file, so treat it as a price floor and not as the cost of a service.
  - **SUB**: a monthly subscription.
  - **WAGE**: employee salary data. This is not a consultancy charge-out rate.

## (c) Review and sign-off by a registered Health and Safety Manager, per file

**No average can be supported. Published data points: 0.** No vendor publishes a separate price for CHSM, CHSO or Pr CHSA review and sign-off. The SACPCMP guideline tariff (2019, rebased 2022) is the official reference. It is either a percentage of construction cost (for Pr CHSA agent services) or time-based, and I could not read its rand values because the gazette PDFs could not be fetched.

The nearest evidenced proxy is a consultancy practitioner rate of **R750 per hour excl VAT** (row 45), or R781 to R1,119 per hour implied by retainers (row 46). This is one vendor group and is not specific to registered CHSMs. Any per-file sign-off figure you publish would need to be your own estimate of hours multiplied by a rate, and should be labelled as an estimate.

## (d) Reliability notes

1. **Snippet-only sourcing.** The egress proxy blocked every page fetch, so every figure is the search engine's reading of the page. Two known problems:
   - Makrosafe's figure appeared as "£4,510", almost certainly rand.
   - MySafetyFile appears as both R400 and R500.

   Verify each URL before publishing.
2. **VAT is mostly unknown.**
   - Stated excl VAT: Absolute Health, AccessPD, SafetyWallet, Makrosafe and one SafetySupplier product.
   - Stated incl VAT: SHE Files and the Coega awards.
   - Everything else: not stated. Do not mix these in a published average without noting it.
3. **Mixed products.** Automated files (about R500), templates (R200 to R3,500) and consultant-compiled files (R2,000 to R10,000) are different products. Averaging them together would mislead. Keep the tiers separate.
4. **Promotions.** Several prices are sale prices (Safety File SA, Key Safety Sector), and I report the sale price. The list prices are about 15 to 40% higher.
5. **Few vendors.** The consultant-compiled construction average rests on 8 prices from 7 vendors. The generic non-construction file rests on 3 prices from 2 vendors. The retainer and audit tiers rest on 1 vendor group each (Absolute Health; AccessPD). That is thin.
6. **Salary data is not charge-out.** PayScale, ERI, Indeed and Talent figures are employee pay. Consultancy charge-out rates (R750+ per hour) are 3 to 10 times higher.
7. **Tender evidence is weak.** I could not open eTenders, municipal award lists or SOE award PDFs beyond Coega. Coega's awards are whole-project SHE services, so they cannot be read as a file price.
8. **Dates.** All rows were retrieved on 23 Sep 2026. Dates were visible only for:
   - Coega awards: Aug 2024
   - Labournet SLA: May 2024 (no price)
   - SafetyWallet risk-assessment blog: Nov 2025 (no rand figure)
   - Indeed salary data: Feb 2026
   - SACPCMP tariff: 2019, rebased 2022

   The rest are current web prices with no publication date.
9. **Suggested next step before publishing:** open the SACPCMP 2019 tariff and the 2022 rebase gazette by hand to get the official Pr CHSA percentages and CHSM/CHSO hourly rates. That is the only authoritative basis for item 3.

## (e) Care Net client price (Director's instruction, 24/09/2026)

This section records a pricing decision, not research. The market figures above are unchanged.

- A Care Net client's signed off File costs a flat **R4 000,00 excluding VAT** (one File for one site), whatever the industry market average.
- A company doing **more than 100 occupational medicals a year** with Care Net gets its signed off File free.
- A site doing **more than 500 medicals a year** with Care Net gets a free signed off File for the whole site and for each of its subcontractors working on that site.
- A sales executive verifies the declared medicals volume before a free tier is confirmed. Multi site and above threshold construction work is still quoted by a sales executive.
- The figures live in vercel/hsf/pricing.js (cnc_client_price, free_medicals_threshold, site_and_subcontractors_threshold) and the rule in hsf/BUILD-CONTRACT.md 15.2. It replaces the earlier "market average less 25%" rule of 23/09/2026.
