Medical Surveillance Plan Assessment

A Medical Surveillance Plan sets out, per job category, exactly which occupational medical assessments your employees need, how often, and on what legal basis. Care Net designs yours from a structured assessment of your operations, correct to South African occupational health legislation for your industry, and reviewed by a registered Occupational Medical Practitioner before anything is released. Approved plans are hosted securely with revision control, and updated as your operations or the law change.

How it works

  1. Enter your details above and follow the secure sign in link we email you. Your assessment is ready the moment you arrive.
  2. Receive your secure, single use assessment link and complete the structured assessment (roughly 30 to 45 minutes with your risk assessment at hand).
  3. Care Net's engine drafts your Plan against the verified regulatory framework for your industry, and a registered Occupational Medical Practitioner reviews, recommends, and signs.
  4. Your approved Plan is delivered for download and hosted securely with revision numbers; updates and revisions follow the same review whenever your operations change.
