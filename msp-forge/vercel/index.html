Medical Surveillance Plan Assessment

A Medical Surveillance Plan sets out, per job category, exactly which occupational medical assessments your employees need, how often, and on what legal basis. Care Net designs yours from a structured assessment of your operations, correct to South African occupational health legislation for your industry, and reviewed by a registered Occupational Medical Practitioner before anything is released. Approved plans are hosted securely with revision control, and updated as your operations or the law change.

Free to build

You can now build your own Medical Surveillance Plan.

Work through the assessment and your Plan is drafted for you at no charge. It sets out, for every job on your site, which medicals your people need, how often, and the regulation behind each one. That is the document you plan from, budget from, and hand to your safety team.

Your Plan comes watermarked. That is the working copy and it is yours to keep. The watermark lifts if you are already a Care Net client, or the day you book your medicals with us. Nothing else is asked of you for that.

If you need it for an audit or a regulator

Then it needs our Occupational Medical Practitioner to read it and sign it, and we would rather tell you why than let you find out later.

Your Plan is drafted by a trained language model working from a framework we maintain, updated every month for new legislation and again for job roles and risk. That framework is strong and we stand behind how it is built. It is still not a person. We will not put a practitioner's name on a document that no person has read, so the practitioner goes through your Plan line by line before signing it. That review is charged per person on the report.

What it comes to depends on your industry and how complex your operation is, so there is no single number we can put here honestly. Work it out for your headcount, and a sales executive confirms the figure before anything starts.

How it works

  1. Enter your details above and follow the secure sign in link we email you. Your assessment is ready the moment you arrive.
  2. Receive your secure, single use assessment link and complete the structured assessment (roughly 30 to 45 minutes with your risk assessment at hand).
  3. Care Net's engine drafts your Plan against the verified regulatory framework for your industry, and a registered Occupational Medical Practitioner reviews, recommends, and signs.
  4. Your approved Plan is delivered for download and hosted securely with revision numbers; updates and revisions follow the same review whenever your operations change.
