CF

Certificates of fitness

Arrives when MyClinicOnline is connected

What will appear

  • Every person in scope, matched on MyClinicOnline’s own person reference, never on a name
  • Each certificate: baseline, periodic and exit, and the construction, professional driving permit and mine certificates where they apply
  • Issue date and expiry, with expiries flagged before they lapse
  • The outcome an employer may hold: fit, fit with a restriction or not fit, with any work restriction
  • The practitioner who signed
TR

Training records

Arrives when MyClinicOnline is connected

What will appear

  • Every training record MyClinicOnline holds for your people
  • Provider, course or unit standard, date completed and expiry
  • The certificate reference for each record
  • The training matrix for every person in scope, which also fills your Health and Safety File
  • Expired records with the renewal path
BK

Bookings

Arrives when MyClinicOnline is connected

What will appear

  • Medicals and training booked with Care Net, with the date and the people booked
  • Renewals falling due, so you can book before a certificate lapses
  • The exact booking details follow the MyClinicOnline interface contract, which is pending

Care Net screens fitness for work and does not diagnose. A certificate of fitness says fit, fit with a restriction or not fit, and no clinical detail ever reaches your portal or your File.
