What is in the File

Fifteen sections. Every element evidenced or flagged.

Every element carries the duty in plain words, the law it rests on, who is responsible, the evidence required, how often it is reviewed and where it stands today: linked from MyClinicOnline, uploaded, outstanding, expired, or not applicable with a written reason. Nothing else.

Your industry adds its own elements on top: for example the full Construction Regulations for building and civils work, the Mine Health and Safety Act regime at a mine, or PSIRA and firearm competency for security services. A universal element is never removed silently; if it does not apply, the File says so and why.

    Linked, not uploaded

    Medicals and training done with Care Net arrive in your File on their own.

    Medicals

    Certificates of fitness per person per protocol, the Construction Regulations medical certificate of fitness where construction work applies, professional driving permit medicals, and the mine certificate of fitness at a mine. Each arrives with its issue date, expiry, any work restriction and the practitioner who signed.

    Only what the employer may lawfully hold: fit, fit with a restriction, or not fit. No clinical detail ever reaches the File.

    Training

    Every training record MyClinicOnline holds for your people, with provider, course or unit standard, date, expiry and certificate reference. It fills the training matrix and the competence evidence of every appointment.

    People are matched on MyClinicOnline's own person reference, never on a name. Where there is no record, the item shows outstanding and you can upload instead; where a record has expired, the File shows the renewal path.

    The law behind your File

    Nothing is cited until it has been checked three times.

    Every instrument in your File is verified against its primary text, corroborated by an independent source, and checked for currency on the day. An instrument that fails a check is left out rather than guessed at, and the element that depends on it shows as awaiting verification instead. The register is reviewed every month; when the law moves, your File opens a revision.

    What the File does, and what it does not.It assembles, evidences, flags and reports. It does not decide that a workplace is safe, certify that a person is competent or determine anyone's fitness for work. The accountable people decide and sign: the Health and Safety Manager signs the safety content, your section 16(2) appointee accepts the File and your chief executive acknowledges it. The Occupational Medical Practitioner signs the medical surveillance plan, which the File holds in Section E as evidence.
