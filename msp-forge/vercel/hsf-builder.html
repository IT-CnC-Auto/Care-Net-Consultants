      Where your documents go

      Held securely, then kept with MyClinicOnline.

      Every document follows the same five steps. You can see each one against every file in the Uploads view of your File. MyClinicOnline is not connected yet, so for now documents go no further than step 3 and stay held securely in Care Net’s staging environment. Steps 4 and 5 follow once the connection goes live.

      1. ↑UploadingYour browser works out the document's SHA 256 fingerprint, then sends it straight to Care Net's private staging storage.
      2. ✓UploadedThe document is in staging, checked against the size you sent, and linked to its section, department and element.
      3. ●Held for MyClinicOnlineThe document waits, encrypted and reachable only by Care Net's servers, until MyClinicOnline takes it.
      4. →Transferred to MyClinicOnlineMyClinicOnline confirms it received a file with the same fingerprint, and keeps it in its secure environment for special personal information.
      5. ×Removed from Care Net stagingOnly then is the staging copy deleted. The name, size and fingerprint stay with your File as its audit trail.

      Your consent and POPIA

      Your documents, handled as special personal information.

      What we ask and why

      Three separate consents, each in full, before a single document is stored: storage in Care Net's secure staging environment for the single purpose of building your File, transfer to MyClinicOnline with the staging copy deleted afterwards, and your confirmation that you may share the documents for your employer.

      Documents go to private, encrypted storage that only Care Net's servers can reach. They are never placed where the public can see them, and the File keeps each document's fingerprint so any change can be detected.

      What the File does, and what it does not

      The File assembles, evidences, flags and reports. It does not decide that a workplace is safe, certify that a person is competent or determine anyone's fitness for work. Care Net screens fitness for work and does not diagnose; a certificate of fitness says fit, fit with a restriction or not fit, and no clinical detail reaches the File.

      A registered health and safety practitioner signs the safety content, your section 16(2) appointee accepts the File and your chief executive acknowledges it. The medical surveillance plan signed by the Occupational Medical Practitioner is filed in Section E as evidence; it is not a sign off of the File. Read the privacy policy.
