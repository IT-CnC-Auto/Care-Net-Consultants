The pilot journey: from sign up to a signed Plan

This page is the complete journey, live. Every step below links into the real system: the same account flow, the same assessment, the same engine, and the same clinical review your Plan goes through. Walk it end to end in about ten minutes.

1Sign in or create your company

One door for everyone: enter your work email, receive a secure sign in link, and either land on your company's status or register a new company on the spot. The moment you are signed in, the system checks two things automatically: an active service level agreement with Care Net, and whether you do 100 or more occupational medicals a year with us. Either one releases your Plan without a watermark.

3Complete the smart assessment

Eleven sections capture everything the engine needs: your sites and contacts, regulatory context, risk assessment and hygiene results, workforce profile, every job category with its hazards and controls, current surveillance arrangements, incident history, your industry from the governed list, your branding (your logo joins the Care Net band on every page of your Plan), POPIA consent, and the declaration. Roughly 30 to 45 minutes with your risk assessment at hand.

The real form opens below in demo preview: everything is visible, nothing is stored, and submission is disabled. Clients receive a secure single use link instead.

Preview the real assessment

4The engine drafts your Plan

Your answers run through the Care Net Method: our maintained, triple verified body of South African occupational health law. It classifies your operation, frames the exact legal instruments that govern it, profiles every job against the hazard map, prescribes the surveillance battery with a legal basis for every test, composes the document, and then checks its own work. Nine checks must pass before a draft exists at all, among them:

  • PASS every citation resolves to a verified instrument
  • PASS every test carries dual justification: the hazard and Employment Equity Act section 7
  • PASS exit medicals are never omitted
  • PASS no unresolved item ever prints; open questions route to the clinical queue

5A registered OMP reviews, recommends, and signs

No Plan releases without a registered Occupational Medical Practitioner. The OMP reviews the draft in the clinical interface, records recommendations, and signs with name and HPCSA number. This gate is enforced in the database itself: there is no pathway to a released document around it. On the working draft package the document stays watermarked and unsigned.

6Delivery, hosting, and revisions

Your signed Plan is delivered for download and hosted securely with revision numbers. When your operations or the law change, the revision runs through the same method and the same clinical review.
