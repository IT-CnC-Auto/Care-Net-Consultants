Twelve items were inspected across eight areas of the site on 18/09/2026. Four passed, four failed, three need watching and one did not apply on the day. Eight risks were rated on the 5 by 5 matrix. Before controls, four were very high; with the controls in this report in place, none is very high and three remain high, each with a corrective action and an owner.

Legal references, in plain words

  • Occupational Health and Safety Act

    The employer must provide and keep a workplace that is safe and without risk to health, as far as is reasonably practicable, and must tell workers about the hazards and how they are controlled. Every finding in this report is read against that duty. The company’s section 16(2) assignee receives this report.

    Findings F-01 to F-12

  • Construction Regulations, 2014

    On a construction site, scaffolds are erected and inspected by a competent person and recorded; ladders are used safely; excavations are shored, barricaded and inspected; falls are planned for; and construction vehicles and mobile plant are operated safely around people.

    Findings F-01, F-02, F-05, F-10, F-12

  • Hazardous Chemical Agents Regulations, 2021

    Hazardous chemicals are listed, stored safely and kept with their safety data sheets, and the people who handle them know the risks and the controls.

    Findings F-06

The law is named in plain words, with no section or regulation numbers other than section 16(2). Each name links to its background in Care Net’s legislation register. The fire equipment finding (F-07) is read against the general duty above and the site’s emergency plan.
