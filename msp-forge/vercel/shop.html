Why the signature is charged and the Plan is not

We would rather tell you than let you find out later.Your Plan is drafted by a trained language model working from a framework we maintain, updated every month for new legislation and again for job roles and risk. That framework is strong and we stand behind how it is built. It is still not a person. We will not put a practitioner's name on a document that no person has read, so the practitioner goes through your Plan line by line before signing it. That review is charged per person on the report.

What it comes to depends on your industry and how complex your operation is, so the figure the calculator gives you is confirmed by a sales executive before anything starts.

Care Net screens fitness for work; we do not diagnose. Findings and referrals rest with the reviewing Occupational Medical Practitioner, and the employer is always the payer, in line with HPCSA guidance.

The law behind your Plan

Seventeen industries. Thirty one verified instruments. Here they are.

Every Plan is drafted against this register. Each instrument is verified three ways before it may be cited: against the primary text, an independent corroborating source, and a currency check that confirms it is still in force. Anything that fails a check is left out rather than guessed at. The register is reviewed every month.
